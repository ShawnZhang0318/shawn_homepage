    张艺桐

    Yitong Zhang

    我是面向机器人系统、具身智能与 AI 产品的工程开发者，具备从机械结构设计、机器人动作开发、系统集成到前后端产品实现的跨栈经验。曾在原力无限（INFIFORCE）深圳研究院担任研究助理，参与家庭服务机器人研发；也曾在香港大学陈佳玉助理教授 Agentic Intelligence Lab担任核心研究实习生（2026.05 - 2026.08）。

    本科毕业于北师香港浸会大学计算机科学与技术专业，目前在香港科技大学攻读人工智能与创业硕士学位。本科期间在 AIRH 支持下创立 Navigator Robotics Lab（约 35 人；战队早期名「烧仙草」），曾主导团队首台哨兵机器人的机械结构设计，并参与 RoboMaster 高校联盟赛。

    机器人之外，我也关注如何把 AI 能力转化为真实产品。我开发的 FundaNotes 饭答，将课件解析、实时同传、上下文问答、智能笔记、思维导图与练习生成串联为完整学习流程。

    更多见 研究、项目、GitHub 与 简历。

    I am an engineer working across robotics systems, embodied AI, and AI products, with experience spanning mechanical design, robot action development, systems integration, and full-stack product delivery. I previously worked as a Research Assistant on home-service robotics at the INFIFORCE Shenzhen Research Institute and as a Core Research Intern in Assistant Professor Jiayu Chen’s Agentic Intelligence Lab at the University of Hong Kong (May 2026 - Aug 2026).

    I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Technology at BNBU and am currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence and Entrepreneurship at HKUST. During my undergraduate studies, with support from AIRH, I founded Navigator Robotics Lab (approximately 35 members; the team was originally named “Shao Xian Cao”), led the mechanical design of its first sentry robot, and competed in RoboMaster.

    Beyond robotics, I care about turning AI capabilities into useful products. My project FundaNotes brings courseware parsing, live interpretation, contextual Q&A, smart notes, mind maps, and practice generation into one learning workflow.
