    研究与技术方向

    Research & Technical Interests

    我的工作连接机械结构、机器人软件、具身智能算法与实际产品。核心问题是：机器人如何理解自然语言需求，将其拆解为可执行动作，并在真实环境中稳定完成长程任务。

    My work connects mechanical design, robotics software, embodied intelligence, and product delivery. The central question is how robots can understand natural-language needs, translate them into executable skills, and reliably complete long-horizon tasks in the physical world.

    具身智能与任务规划

    Embodied AI & Task Planning

    曾在 原力无限（INFIFORCE）深圳研究院担任研究助理，参与家庭服务机器人研发；也曾在香港大学陈佳玉助理教授 Agentic Intelligence Lab担任核心研究实习生（2026.05 - 2026.08）。原力无限期间的工作聚焦从自然语言需求到真实机器人执行的完整链路，包括指令理解、任务拆解、动作函数生成与调用、导航、失败恢复和执行反馈。

    • 面向家庭服务场景构建可组合、可复用的机器人动作能力。
    • 以显式任务状态和工具调用约束长流程，连接大模型推理与底层机器人接口。
    • 围绕“需求理解—任务规划—动作执行—反馈恢复”构建真机闭环。
    • 关注具身智能体在不确定真实环境中的规划、恢复与持续执行。

    I previously worked as a Research Assistant on home-service robotics at the INFIFORCE Shenzhen Research Institute and as a Core Research Intern in Assistant Professor Jiayu Chen's Agentic Intelligence Lab at HKU from May to August 2026. My INFIFORCE work focused on end-to-end language-to-action pipelines spanning instruction understanding, task decomposition, action-function generation and calls, navigation, recovery, and execution feedback.

    • Composable, reusable robot skills for home-service scenarios.
    • Explicit task states and tool calls that connect model reasoning with low-level robot interfaces.
    • A real-robot loop from intent understanding and task planning to physical execution and recovery.
    • Planning, recovery, and persistent execution under real-world uncertainty.

    • 使用 SolidWorks 进行机械结构设计、关键部件建模、装配验证与选材。
    • 完成 ROS 通信、导航定位、动作接口与机器人系统集成。
